## 7.4.0 (unreleased)

This release is additions: the container region, background panels, Panel-look panels that follow resource packs, and a placement per game mode. Nothing was removed, and mods built for 7.3.0 work as before; the forms with no game mode are deprecated. A resource pack that themed MenuKit's buttons through `menukit:panel_raised` themes them through `menukit:control_raised` now.

### Added

- One scrolling column above the inventory on container screens. `ContainerRegion.section(id, scope)` adds a section to the screen's container region: the chest's own grid, a box opened below it, another below that, stacked in one column above the player's inventory. There is one region per screen, however many mods add to it. The screen becomes two panels, each with its own frame: the container list, with its scrollbar inside its frame, and the inventory panel below it, which never scrolls. The list grows before it scrolls: while the two panels fit, they stay centered on the screen as one block, the list's top moving up and the inventory panel down by half the growth each (on a window too short for vanilla's own margins, the side with less room holds until the block is centered), and only once they reach the screen's top and bottom margins does the list scroll, by pixels, with the shared scrollbar; a resize lays it out again, and it is never shorter than the screen's own container part. The screen's `leftPos`, `topPos`, `imageWidth` and `imageHeight` are the rectangle around both panels whenever the layout changes, so panels on the menu frame, other mods' buttons and recipe viewers go around both, and a click in the gap between them with an item held drops nothing; vanilla's inventory slots and label go with the inventory panel, and a mod reading `leftPos + slot.x` still lands on the slot. `ContainerRegion.CONTAINERS` and `ContainerRegion.INVENTORY` are anchors for each panel. The wheel scrolls the region from anywhere on the screen, but only on a turn nothing else used: over a slot a mod that moves items with the wheel keeps it, and so does a recipe viewer's list. A shift-click into one of its slots scrolled out of view scrolls there, and carrying an item to its top or bottom edge scrolls it, faster nearer the edge. Both panels are the screen's background: everything else on the screen, other mods' buttons and recipe viewers included, draws over them and over the region's slots, and they block none of it. While the region is up, the screen's background still runs, other mods' hooks in it included; only vanilla's own container texture and the screen's title are left out, the panels and the sections' titles in their place. [Concepts](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/concepts.md#container-region), [recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#stack-containers-in-one-scrolling-column-above-the-inventory).

- The container region lays the screen out again when its layout changes while the screen is open, the way a window resize does (`Screen.resize` at the screen's own size): every mod adds its widgets again from its own init hook, at the new bounds, so another mod's button by the screen's edges follows the panel it was by when a section opens or closes. It never happens on a scroll, and happens at most once a frame however many sections changed. What a resize keeps (the item on the cursor, the scroll, open sections, the anvil's name) is kept. [Concepts](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/concepts.md#container-region).

- A container region section shows only while its content is visible, so content that hides itself, such as a slot library's element for a slot group not on the open menu, takes its section with it. `ContainerRegion.Builder.title(...)` draws a section's title where vanilla draws a container's. Where a resource pack blanks that title while other sections show, the section is called by its container, so the player can still tell the containers apart: its source's custom name or its block's name ("Lime Shulker Box"), in the player's language, or `name(...)` for a section with no block behind it. [Recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#stack-containers-in-one-scrolling-column-above-the-inventory).

- Panels behind everything. `Panel.Builder.background()` makes a panel part of the screen's own background: it draws where the screen draws its image, so the screen's widgets, labels and slots, every other mod's widgets and every other panel draw over it. It claims nothing, so whatever is over it stays live, and its own elements get a click only after the screen's widgets declined it and the wheel only after everything else declined it. Slots it shows draw with it, right after it, clipped to it: over the panel, under every widget, so a button over one hides it, and over the button the slot is not hovered. Every slot still goes through the screen's own `extractSlot`, so other mods' slot marks still show. [Recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#a-panel-behind-everything-on-a-container-screen).

- Panel-look panels follow the resource pack. A raised panel in the Panel look, on a container screen, on a mod's own Panel-look screen or as a dialog, takes its frame, fill and slot frames from the pack's chest art, cut from the texture and tiled, never stretched, at any resolution. In the container region each section's part of the list takes its own container texture, so a shulker box in a chest's list looks as it does on its own screen, and the inventory panel takes the inventory half of the screen's texture. Art that can't be cut cleanly, such as a textured fill, keeps MenuKit's look, and with no pack nothing changes. Mods have nothing to do. [Resource packs](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/resource-packs.md).

- Panel-look controls follow the pack's restyled vanilla widgets. Buttons, toggles, dropdowns, menu buttons, the color field's swatch and the item dialog's controls draw vanilla's `widget/button` sprites where a pack restyled them, with vanilla's label colors, and checkboxes draw vanilla's checkbox. A pack can theme MenuKit directly instead with `menukit:panel_raised` and the new `menukit:control_raised`, `control_raised_highlighted`, `control_raised_pressed` and `control_raised_disabled`, which win over vanilla's. Every state of a control comes from the same source as its normal state, and a state the source has no sprite for is derived from the normal one, so a control never changes look between states. [Resource packs](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/resource-packs.md#sprites-a-pack-can-override).

- Text on a Panel-look panel reads on any pack's fill. Heading, body and muted text keep their colors wherever they stand out from the fill (4.5:1 for body text) and take a color made from the fill where they don't. On a dark-mode pack whose text shader repaints vanilla's label gray, MenuKit's gray is left for the shader. A custom element gets this through `AbstractPanelElement.textColor`.

- A container region section can name the container it shows. `ContainerRegion.Builder.source(...)` names it, with `ContainerRegion.Source.of(BlockEntity)` for a placed one and `Source.of(ItemStack)` for one opened from its item (an item that isn't a block gives no block). The section takes its container texture and its name from it. A section without one is the screen's own container, the block under the crosshair as the screen opened when it holds a container of the screen's size and the screen has its title.

- Per-section container art through OptiGUI. With OptiGUI 2.x installed, MenuKit evaluates the pack's OptiGUI and OptiFine rules for each container region section against its source, so a lime box below a chest has the lime art and the chest keeps its own. Without OptiGUI the rules aren't read. [Recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#a-shulker-box-from-an-item-and-one-placed-in-the-world-each-in-its-own-art).

- `ContainerRegion.Builder.texture(...)` names the container texture a section stands for where no source's block gives one, such as a mod's own slot group: `ContainerRegion.CHEST` by default, or `ContainerRegion.SHULKER_BOX`. A source's block wins: a shulker box gives the shulker box's texture, a chest, ender chest or barrel the chest's. `ContainerRegion.GAP` is the room between two sections and `ContainerRegion.CONTENT_WIDTH` a section's width before the scrollbar. `Panel.isBackground()` says whether a panel was built with `background()`.

- A placement per game mode. `ModeScope` is `SURVIVAL`, `CREATIVE` or `BOTH`, from the player's infinite materials (creative is a player with them), read every frame, so `/gamemode` with a screen open takes effect on the next frame. `Panel.Builder.in(scope, placement)` says where a panel goes in each mode, once per scope; each frame the panel takes the current mode's placement, else its `BOTH` one, and with neither it doesn't show in that mode. `Placement.screen(position)` is against the host's own reference, `Placement.around(anchor, position)` in the regions around an anchor's box, and `Placement.at(anchor, dx, dy)` at the box's top-left plus an offset, at natural size, with no stacking, budget or clamp. A screen adapter puts a panel's anchored placements in their anchors' hosts on the screens it targets, and an `AnchorPanelAdapter` needs no `on(...)` when the placements name their anchors. `Panel.placements()` lists them, and `getPosition()` is the position in effect now. `HudPanel.builder(id, scope)`, `OwnHost.add(panel, padding)` and `ContainerRegion.section(id, scope)` are the scoped forms. `Panel.Builder.fixed()` marks a panel the player can't move. [Concepts](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/concepts.md#mode-scope), [recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#put-a-panel-somewhere-else-in-creative).

### Changed

- The container region is never on the player's own inventory, survival's or creative's, whatever a section's targeting says. The player's inventory slots it moves are placed every frame from the slots the menu holds, so a slot another mod puts in while the screen is open goes with the inventory panel too, and closing the screen moves back exactly the slots that were moved.
- `SlotClip.panelShowing(slot)` keeps the panel that last showed a slot by the slot's container, index and class, so a slot made again for the same place, such as creative's inventory slots on a tab switch, keeps its answer.
- A click on one of a container screen's widgets is never eaten as a click in a slot panel's gap or on the hidden part of a slot cut by a scroll edge: the widget gets it.
- An element wholly outside the clip it is drawn in is skipped at every depth, not only a `ScrollContainer`'s own children: a row of slots in a `Flow` inside a scroll area is not drawn while it is out of view, so its slot library parks it and nothing scrolled away sits over what is past the edge, such as the inventory below a scrolling grid. An element with an open popover is still drawn.
- Raised controls in the Panel look draw MenuKit's new `menukit:control_raised` sprites instead of `menukit:panel_raised`, so a pack themes panels and controls each on its own. A pack that overrode `panel_raised` to restyle MenuKit's buttons now overrides `control_raised`. With no pack they look as before.
- The pressed look on a held button, MenuKit's own and vanilla's, is one transform drawn relative to the button's art, so it reads on a pack's dark or colored buttons as well as on vanilla's. On vanilla's own button it matches the old pressed look to within a few levels along the bevel.
- `SlotRendering.drawSlotBackground` draws an 18 by 18 slot on a Panel-look panel as the pack's slot, cut from its container art or its restyled `container/slot`. Elsewhere it draws as before.

### Deprecated

These keep working, mean `ModeScope.BOTH`, and are removed at 8.0.0. [Upgrading](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/upgrading-7.md#to-740).

- `Panel.Builder.position(position)`: use `in(ModeScope.BOTH, Placement.screen(position))`.
- `AnchorPanelAdapter.on(anchors)`: put the anchor in the placement, `Placement.around(anchor, position)`.
- `HudPanel.builder(id)`: use `HudPanel.builder(id, scope)`.
- `OwnHost.add(panel, position, padding)`: use `OwnHost.add(panel, padding)`, which reads the panel's placements.

## 7.3.0

This release is additions, new names for the two looks, and one change to when slots shown in a panel are drawn. Nothing was removed, and mods built for 7.2.0 work as before.

### Added

- Lists that change while the screen is open. `ListView` shows one row per item of a list your mod owns and follows it every frame: a new item gets its row at once, a removed one loses it, and every other row stays as it is, so a rename in progress keeps its text and the keyboard while the list refreshes. Rows are kept by a key you give, in your list's order. An optional selection, held by your mod, is set by clicking a row and moved with the arrow keys, and a row your code selects scrolls into view. A list in the middle of a page pushes what is below it down as rows are added or grow, and back up as they go, in the same frame: place what follows it as if it took no room, as after a closed `Section`. [Recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#a-list-that-changes-while-the-screen-is-open).
- Editing a list. `ListView` takes optional builder calls, and a list that uses none of them is unchanged. `add(label, action)` puts an add button per kind in the list's toolbar, and the new row is scrolled into view; `add(label, tooltip, action)` gives the button a hover tooltip saying what the kind is, fixed or read while hovered, and `remove(label, tooltip, ...)` does the same for the remove button. `remove(...)` adds a "- Remove item" button and `move(...)` up and down arrows, both acting on the selected row and gray where they can't act; `pinned(...)` rows can't be removed or moved, and `confirmRemove(...)` asks first, naming the item. After a remove the next row is selected, and Alt+Up and Alt+Down move the selected row. `search(...)` adds a search field and `sort(...)` a sort picker with a direction button. `controlsInRows()` puts up, down and a remove cross in each row instead, for short lists like a config list of text fields. `addDisabledWhen(...)` grays the add buttons and `removeDisabledWhen(...)` the remove, the toolbar's and each row's cross, for a list at its longest or shortest. `disabledReason(...)` explains a grayed list. The toolbar is `list.toolbar()`, placed above the scroll area so it stays put; it is as wide as its container gives it, so its right end is the scroll area's outer edge, while a toolbar the list draws at its own top lines up with the rows. `title(...)` puts the list's heading on the toolbar's line, at its left edge, so the heading and the buttons share one line; read every frame when given a supplier, so it can show a live count, and cut with "..." before the buttons. `title(title, tooltip)` gives the heading a hover tooltip, such as a config option's description, fixed or read while hovered; it shows instead of the whole heading when the heading is cut. The selected row is now a lighter card in the Panel look, and keeps its outline in the Default look. [Recipes](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#a-list-the-player-adds-to-removes-from-and-reorders).
- A dialog that edits a copy. `EditDialog` shows a title, your controls and Cancel and Save, laid out like every MenuKit dialog. The controls edit a draft, so nothing changes until Save, and Cancel or Escape drops it. Open it from any button of your own, such as an Edit button in a list row. [Recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#edit-a-row-in-a-dialog-from-its-own-button).
- `Section.builder(...)` and `Section.Builder.swatch(...)` also take a supplier, read every frame, so a section built once, such as a `ListView` row, shows a renamed title or a new swatch color without being rebuilt. A title too long for its header is cut with "..." before a list row's controls, and shown whole when you hover it.
- `ConfirmDialog.Builder.title(...)` and `body(...)` also take a supplier, read while the dialog shows, so one dialog can name whichever item it's about.
- `Flow.Builder.crossAlign(CrossAlign.CENTER)` centers a row's children on its line, and `END` sets them on its bottom, for a label beside a taller control.
- A button with a menu of actions. `MenuButton` opens a small menu whose rows run actions, for a row's Duplicate and Delete. An action that destroys something is drawn red. The menu is never cut off by a scroll area around it.
- A list that scrolls under a fixed header. `ScrollContainer.Builder.fillHeight()` takes the height it is given: the rest of a screen's main panel, of a vertical `Flow` under a title and buttons, or of a `Tabs` page. A `Tabs` page whose body fills hands the page to it instead of scrolling the whole body, so the header stays put and only the list moves, and the page looks the same as before.
- Text fields that can be canceled and started from code. Escape on a focused `TextField` puts back the text from before the edit, lets go of the keyboard and runs `onCancel`, without closing the screen. `focus()` gives a field the keyboard, for a rename field that appears with its button.
- `Dropdown` and `DropdownMulti` can take their options from a supplier, for options that change while the screen is open.
- `ScreenMatcher.where(test)` puts a panel on the container screens a test accepts, asked as each screen opens, for a panel that belongs where a slot group's slots are. [Recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#show-a-panel-on-the-screens-that-hold-your-slots).
- Item rules that leave chosen components out. `ItemMatch.ignoring(DataComponents.DAMAGE)` makes an `EXACT` or `CONTAINS` rule look past those component types on both sides, so a rule for one exact sword still matches it once it is worn. `ignoringCount()` leaves the count out of `EXACT`. `ItemPicker.Builder.ignoring(...)` and `ignoringCount()` declare the same once for every rule the picker writes, and with `ignoringCount()` the Edit tab has no count. `ItemMatch.CODEC` saves both, and rules saved before load ignoring nothing. [Item picker](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/concepts.md#item-picker).
- `Attachment`, the one way a container attaches a child added after it was built, for mods that write their own containers.
- For slot libraries, `SlotClip.panelShowing(slot)` says whether the panel that last showed a slot is on screen this frame, so a vanilla slot whose panel is hidden can go back to its own place while one scrolled out of view parks.
- `Panel.placedBounds()` returns a panel's own rectangle on the current screen, or `null` when it isn't placed right now. Use it to put something against a panel of your own, such as a close button in its corner, with `PanelPosition.pixel(...)`. It needs no `Anchor` declaration; it's read fresh each call.
- `HudCycleStrip.begin()` and the new `slideProgress(SlideStart)` and `slideDone(SlideStart)` time a mod's own slide against the strip's. `SlideStart` carries its own clock, so there is no raw millis value left to stamp with the wrong one.
- A HUD status column. `HudPanel.Builder.statusColumn()` puts a HUD panel in the 20 px gap between the hearts and the hunger bar, just above the XP level number, or just above the hotbar where the game mode shows no status bars. Panels there stack upward by priority, 2 px apart, and a hidden one takes no room. While anything shows in the column, the chat, vanilla's held-item name, and the action bar message move up only as far as the column reaches them. `HudPanel.SlotBuilder.scale(...)` draws a HUD slot smaller, so a slot at 0.8 fits the column.

### Changed

- The two looks are now called the Default look and the Panel look. `ScreenKind.OPTIONS` is now `ScreenKind.DEFAULT`, `ScreenKind.CONTAINER` is now `ScreenKind.PANEL`, and `ScrollContainer.OPTIONS_INSET_X` and `OPTIONS_INSET_Y` are now `DEFAULT_INSET_X` and `DEFAULT_INSET_Y`. The old names still work and mean the same looks, but are deprecated and go in 8.0.0. `ScreenKind.values()` lists only the two new names, and a `switch` over `ScreenKind` must name them. A mod that lets the player switch look offers a toggle labeled "Look: Default" or "Look: Panel".
- A `ScrollContainer` draws its scrollbar only while its content is taller than it, in both looks; before, the Panel look showed an empty track with a grayed handle. One built with `fitWidth()` gives its children the scrollbar's width while it is hidden, so they reach its right edge. Children you place yourself keep the width you gave them.
- A `ScrollContainer` pushes the children below one that grows (a `ListView` a row is added to, an open `Section`, a wrapped label) down by its growth, and back up as it shrinks, as a panel does, and its scroll range follows in the same frame. Place what follows a list as if it took no room. Scrolled down, a child above the view that grows or shrinks moves the scroll by as much, so what is on screen stays put. A `ScrollContainer` built with `fitHeight()` is as tall as its content up to its declared height, and now pushes what is below it the same way as it grows and shrinks: place what follows it as if it took no room.
- A slot a panel shows draws right after that panel, on top of it. Before, a vanilla slot shown in a panel was drawn before the panel, so the panel covered its item. A panel above another now covers the slots shown in the one below it, and a slot in a dialog draws on the dialog in the same frame. Slots no panel shows draw as before. Mods that mark slots by hooking vanilla's `extractSlot` still see every slot. A mod that hooks the whole slot pass (`extractSlots`) sees the slots shown in panels drawn after it.
- A panel placed by an adapter whose elements are all hidden or empty is no longer drawn as an empty frame, and no longer blocks clicks. A panel with a pinned size keeps its room as before.
- The cycle strip's slide and MK's HUD notifications time themselves off vanilla's own clock (`Util.getMillis()`) instead of the wall clock, so a client gametest that pins and steps the clock can record an animation frame by frame. `HudCycleStrip.slideProgress(long)` and `slideDone(long)` still read `Util.getMillis()` as before, but are deprecated: a `startMillis` from any other clock, `System.currentTimeMillis()` included, silently never reports done, since the two clocks don't share a zero. Use `begin()` and the new overloads instead.

### Fixed

- An element removed from a screen lets go of the keyboard if it had it, and an open dropdown in it closes.
- A button whose click opens another screen no longer looks pressed the next time its panel is shown. Leaving a screen now ends every element's press, drag and keyboard focus, even when the mouse release never arrives (vanilla drops input while it leaves and loads a world).
- A `Tabs` sidebar whose tabs scroll no longer cuts labels that fit when they don't ("List..."). The scrollbar's lane now widens the column instead of narrowing the tabs, so the body is that much narrower while the tabs scroll, in both looks. The sidebar header sits over the tabs, right of the lane. Labels are cut only when the widest is wider than a third of the `Tabs`, as before.
- A side-scroll `Tabs` tab wider than the room between the arrows is narrowed to it and its label shortened with "...", instead of being clipped at the arrows.
