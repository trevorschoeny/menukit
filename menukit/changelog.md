## 7.2.0

Additions only. Nothing was removed, and mods built for 7.1.0 work as before.

### Added

- A colour setting. `ColorField` shows a swatch of a colour and opens a dialog when clicked. The first tab holds Minecraft's 16 dye colours in rainbow order, shown by their dye items and named by colour. The second is a full picker: a saturation and brightness square, a hue bar, a transparency bar when the value has alpha, and a hex field that only accepts valid hex. Changes show at once, and Cancel puts back the colour from before. The value is an int, `0xRRGGBB`, or `0xAARRGGBB` with `alpha(true)`. The swatch and its dialog draw like the rest of the screen, on an inventory and on an options screen. [Recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#a-colour-setting).
- Item settings that hold whole stacks, and lists of them. `ItemPicker` gains `stack`, `stacks`, `rule` and `rules` beside its 6.1.0 `state` for one item. `match(...)` picks how stacks compare. `ITEM` keeps the kind of item only, `EXACT` the exact stack, and `CONTAINS` the item and at least the components set, so an empty rule means a plain item for `EXACT` and any variant for `CONTAINS`. `playerChoosesMatch()` lets the player pick Any or Exactly per slot. One shared matcher, `ItemMatch`, compares stacks the way vanilla's item predicates do, so no mod writes its own, and `ItemMatch.CODEC` saves a rule. A list shows as a wrapping row of slots with an empty one to add to. [Recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#an-item-setting).
- A new item dialog. Clicking an item picker opens a dialog over the screen, in its look, instead of a separate chooser screen. The setting's slots sit at the top. The Search tab's box takes typing at once. It searches every item from the creative inventory's search tab, with each potion, enchanted book level and other variant as its own entry. A click on an item puts it in the selected slot, which stays selected. Each filled slot, in the dialog and on the page, has a small remove button in its corner that empties it. In a list, the items after it move up. The Edit tab edits the selected slot's name, count, enchantments and any other component as text in `/give` syntax, checked by vanilla's own parser with its errors. The enchantments are in two lists. Under Added are those on the item, each with its level, a `-` and a `+` (from I to its highest) and a remove button. Under Not Added are those that fit the item and can be added, each with an add button. The ones that conflict with one already on it are greyed and say what they conflict with. The dialog is as wide and as tall as the tab it shows, so the whole grid always shows. Cancel puts back the value from before. The dialog never touches the player's inventory.
- A home for widgets made elsewhere. `WidgetHost` puts any vanilla-style widget, such as a custom control another config library hands over, in a panel. It moves the widget with the panel and its scroll, clips it to its box, passes it clicks, drags, the wheel and typed keys, and gives it keyboard focus and narration. A widget that opens a list under itself can draw over everything with `overflow()`. [Recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#host-a-widget-another-library-made).
- A `Tabs` the size of the tab it shows. `fitShownTab()` on a `Tabs` with no fixed size makes it as wide and as tall as the shown tab instead of the largest, so a dialog holding it resizes when you switch tabs and its buttons follow. The colour dialog and the item dialog use it.
- Elements that size to what they hold. `ScrollContainer.Builder.fitWidth()` (with `height(h)`) makes a scroll area as wide as its content plus its scrollbar, taking more room when its panel gives it. `fitHeight()` makes it as tall as its content up to its height. `TextField.Builder.fill()` and `TextLabel.Builder.fill()` take the width they are given instead of setting it, for a field that spans a dialog or a message that wraps rather than widening it. A filling field asks for at least its hint's width.
- Radio buttons are round, so a set of them never reads as checkboxes.
- Cards in a list. `Flow.Builder.boxed()` draws a card behind a Flow's children, `BOX_PADDING` (6 px) inside it. On an options screen the card is a dark translucent box with a light border that brightens on hover, and on an inventory it is the inset panel look. A boxed row fills its width, so a list of them lines up like vanilla's world list.
- A sidebar menu without its tabs. `tabColumn(false)` on a `SIDEBAR` `Tabs` leaves out the column and gives the page the full width. A settings screen for one mod can then look exactly like a tabbed one: the same darker page, insets, bottom line, scrollbar and header across the top.

### Fixed

- Dialogs have the same margin on every side. `ConfirmDialog`, `AlertDialog` and the colour dialog leave 4 px between the frame and their content on all four sides, and the buttons sit centred between the content above them and the frame below. Before, the content sat 8 px further from the left and top of the frame than from the right and bottom. `ConfirmDialog.PADDING` and `SECTION_GAP` (and `AlertDialog`'s) are now 4.
- Inside a dialog on an options screen, a scroll area's list band and a `Tabs` page inset their content by the dialog's own 4 px gap rather than the full screen's 14 px. The full-screen inset on top of the dialog's padding left wide empty margins. A panel hands down whether it is a modal (`PanelElement.inDialog`) and the kind of screen it is on (`PanelElement.screenKind`) to its elements. `ScrollContainer.bandInsetX`, `bandInsetY` and `DIALOG_INSET` are the one rule.
- A `RAISED` panel on an options screen keeps 4 px between vanilla's popup frame and its elements, as it does inside MenuKit's frame on an inventory screen. Before, its content came within 1 px of the frame. The panel is 6 px bigger each way.
- A `Tabs` with no fixed size that grows after it is placed, for example on an options screen's page, pushes the elements below it down instead of covering them.
- A `TextField` or `Slider` on a panel over a vanilla screen, such as the inventory, now takes clicks. Before, they only worked on a mod's own screen.
- A `TextField` or `Slider` whose panel is hidden, or whose tab is not shown, now lets go of keyboard focus instead of taking keys out of sight, and Tab skips it.
