## 7.1.0

### Added

- The cycle strip. A small hotbar drawn just right of the vanilla one shows a ring of stacks turning through the held slot, and slides one cell with a little spring each time the ring turns. Any mod that rotates stacks into the hand can register a source with `HudCycleStrip`, so its strip shows without needing any other mod installed. A ring can read as a row, as a column rising above the hotbar, or as both in a cross that shares the held cell. [The guide](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/cycle-strip.md) has the details.
- HUD panels beside the hotbar. On the HUD, `HudPanel.builder(...).region(OutsideRegion.X)` places a panel around the vanilla hotbar: `LEFT_ALIGN_BOTTOM` is a column of icons just left of it, rising from its bottom. Panels in one region stack 2 pixels apart in priority order, and a hidden one takes no room, so small icons from several mods line up beside the hotbar without overlapping or leaving gaps. While vanilla draws the offhand slot beside the hotbar, the regions on its side start beyond the slot, so an icon never covers it. [Recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#put-an-icon-beside-the-hotbar).
- Hide item tooltips and other tooltips separately. `MKTooltip.hideWhen` now also takes a predicate of `MKTooltip.Kind`, asked once per tooltip with `ITEM` or `OTHER`, so a mod can offer "hide item tooltips" and "hide all other tooltips" as two settings. Item tooltips from slots, the creative menu, the recipe book, items in chat and other mods drawing items through vanilla's item tooltip are `ITEM`. The existing `hideWhen(() -> ...)` still hides both. [Recipe](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/recipes.md#hide-item-tooltips-separately).
- A gap per panel. `PanelPosition.region(...)` and `screenAnchor(...)` take `.gap(n)`, the space in pixels before that panel: off the frame (or anchor box, or hotbar) and off the sibling before it in a region, off the sibling before it on a screen spot. `.gap(0)` lets panels sit flush, so slot panels stacked on a slot group keep vanilla's 18 pixel pitch. Panels that do not call it stay exactly where they were (2 pixels in a region, the host's spacing on a spot). `HudPanel.builder(...).gap(n)` sets it on a HUD panel. A negative gap throws when the position is built.
- Tab icons. `Tabs.tab(id).icon(texture, textureSize, drawSize)` draws a picture left of a tab's label, in every mode. The texture is any standalone square texture, such as a mod's icon loaded at runtime, and is read every frame. `icon(sprite, drawSize)` uses a GUI sprite instead. The tab widens to fit the icon, the icon and label are centred or aligned together, and an options screen's underline stays under the label. Tabs without an icon look exactly as before. [Concepts](https://github.com/trevorschoeny/menukit/blob/main/docs/concepts.md#tabs).
- Icons from a texture. `Icon.builder().texture(id, textureSize)` shows a standalone square texture, drawn whole at the icon's size, alongside the existing `sprite(...)`.
- Tooltips of several lines. `tooltip(first, more...)` on every element builder and on `Panel` takes the lines one by one, each in its own style, and `MKTooltip.joinLines(lines)` makes one text of them for a tooltip read every frame.

### Fixed

- On an `MKScreen` or custom menu screen, hiding one of the panels stacked below the main panel no longer leaves a 12 pixel hole: the next panel closes up to 14 pixels below the last one shown, as when nothing is hidden.
- A line feed (`\n`) in a tooltip now starts a new line on every tooltip. Before, only a tooltip wide enough to wrap broke its lines, and a short one drew the line feed as a boxed "LF" on a single line. It works in plain text, in a styled part of the text and in a translation, and each line keeps its style.

Nothing was removed. Mods built for 7.0.0 work as before.
